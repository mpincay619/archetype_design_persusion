# Unity Persuasion Principle

[Back to Persuasion](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
Introduced by Robert Cialdini in his book *Pre-Suasion* as the seventh principle of influence, Unity describes shared identity. It is not merely about finding common interests (Liking); it is about "We"-ness—being part of the same tribe, family, heritage, or exclusive community.

## How do you recognize or use it?
- **Characteristics:** Language emphasizing kinship ("One of us," "Our community," "Together"), insider terminology, co-creation opportunities, and badges of shared belonging.
- **Audience & Context:** Lifestyle brands, membership organizations, collaborative crowdfunding, and subscription communities where belonging drives brand commitment.

## Examples

### Application Example: Sustainable Brand Community Co-Creation
A minimalist white t-shirt brand invites verified repeat buyers into an exclusive "Core Circle." Rather than standard commercial discounts, members vote directly on upcoming fabric weights, receive custom monogrammed numbering inside their collars, and share photos on a curated communal journal. Framing the relationship as shared stewardship rather than buyer-seller interaction deepens customer retention.
