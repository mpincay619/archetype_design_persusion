# Vaporwave

[Back to Postmodernism](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
Vaporwave emerged in the early 2010s as an internet-native postmodern art and music genre. It repurposes 1980s and 1990s consumer technology, corporate elevator music, early 3D rendering, and commercial advertising into an ambient critique of techno-optimism and consumer culture.

## How do you recognize or use it?
- **Characteristics:** Pastel neon gradients (cyan, pink, lavender), classical Greco-Roman marble busts, early Windows 95/98 UI elements, low-poly wireframes, glitch artifacts, and Japanese typography.
- **Audience & Context:** Youth subcultures, digital fashion, music apparel, retro gaming, and alternative internet brands looking for nostalgic irony.

## Examples

### Example 1: Visual Album Art
![Floral Shoppe Album Cover](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/0/0d/Macintosh_Plus_-_Floral_Shoppe_cover.jpg/300px-Macintosh_Plus_-_Floral_Shoppe_cover.jpg)  
*Image credit: Beer on the Rug / Vektroid*

**Floral Shoppe Album Artwork (2011), Vektroid (Macintosh Plus).** Combining the bust of Helios with a pink checkered grid and Japanese lettering, this cover established the quintessential visual vocabulary of vaporwave.

### Example 2: Conceptual Digital Media
![Far Side Virtual Artwork](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/8/87/James_Ferraro_-_Far_Side_Virtual.jpg/300px-James_Ferraro_-_Far_Side_Virtual.jpg)  
*Image credit: Hippos in Tanks / James Ferraro*

**Far Side Virtual (2011), James Ferraro.** Featuring floating iPad interfaces, corporate stock art, and synthetic surfaces, Ferraro's package serves as a satirical monument to ubiquitous corporate digital design.
