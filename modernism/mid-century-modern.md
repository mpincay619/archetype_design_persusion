# Mid-Century Modern

[Back to Modernism](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
Mid-Century Modern design flourished roughly from 1945 to 1969. It emphasized functional simplicity, organic curves paired with clean lines, and democratic mass-production following World War II, bringing elevated design into everyday consumer homes.

## How do you recognize or use it?
- **Characteristics:** Organic biomorphic curves, warm natural woods (teak, walnut) juxtaposed with molded plastics and metals, uncluttered open layouts, and restrained earthy/warm palettes.
- **Audience & Context:** Residential interiors, lifestyle branding, minimal furniture, and friendly, accessible consumer interfaces.

## Examples

### Example 1: Furniture Design
![Eames Lounge Chair](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/14/Eames_Lounge_and_Ottoman_White.jpg/800px-Eames_Lounge_and_Ottoman_White.jpg)  
*Image credit: Wikimedia Commons / Herman Miller Archive*

**Eames Lounge Chair and Ottoman (1956), Charles and Ray Eames.** Crafted from molded plywood shells and supple leather, this chair redefined modern domestic comfort by marrying industrial technology with warm organic ergonomics. [MoMA Collection](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/2851).

### Example 2: Corporate Graphic Identity
![IBM 8-Bar Logo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/51/IBM_logo.svg/800px-IBM_logo.svg.png)  
*Image credit: Wikimedia Commons / Paul Rand*

**IBM Graphic Identity (1956/1972), Paul Rand.** Rand's bold striped lettering replaced ornate corporate emblems with a clean, mathematically paced visual system symbolizing speed and technological clarity. [Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum](https://collection.cooperhewitt.org).
