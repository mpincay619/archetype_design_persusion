# Sage

[Back to this section](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

## What is it?
The Sage is a brand archetype centered on truth, knowledge, analysis, and objective wisdom. In Margaret Mark and Carol S. Pearson's framework, its core promise is clarity and understanding: finding the truth that sets people free.

## How do you recognize or use it?
Look for an objective, thoughtful voice; data-driven explanations; clean, dignified aesthetics; and clear information architecture. It suits educational platforms, research institutes, analytics tools, and premium craftsmanship where material science matters.

## Examples

### Example 1: Modernist (Mid-Century Modern)
![Forma Sage Mid-Century Modern Hero](../assets/heroes/sage/modernist.svg)

* **Archetype:** Sage
* **Design Style:** [Mid-Century Modern](../modernism/mid-century-modern.md)
* **Persuasion Principle:** [Authority](../persuasion/authority.md)
* **Headline:** The Science of Pure Fabric.
* **Supporting Copy:** Researched, laboratory-tested 280 GSM combed weave for thermal balance and longevity.
* **CTA Button:** **Read the Fabric Whitepaper** — opens textile test documentation.
* **Design Rationale:** Structured typography and organic layout communicate clarity and intellectual rigor. Authority framing highlights verified product data.

### Example 2: Postmodernist (Vaporwave)
![Forma Sage Vaporwave Hero](../assets/heroes/sage/postmodernist.svg)

* **Archetype:** Sage
* **Design Style:** [Vaporwave](../postmodernism/vaporwave.md)
* **Persuasion Principle:** [Consistency & Commitment](../persuasion/consistency.md)
* **Headline:** Knowledge Refined Over Time.
* **Supporting Copy:** An archival aesthetic designed for those who value timeless insight over temporary trends.
* **CTA Button:** **Explore the Archive** — opens the brand philosophy library.
* **Design Rationale:** Retro digital motifs critique fleeting culture while consistency appeals to the consumer's self-image as a thoughtful buyer.
