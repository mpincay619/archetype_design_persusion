## What I learned
I learned that design research needs to be specific and source-based, not just stylistic. For example, while working on the De Stijl page, I had to distinguish the movement’s core ideas—order, asymmetry, and geometric abstraction—from broader modernist themes so the explanation stayed accurate and grounded in the historical evidence.
